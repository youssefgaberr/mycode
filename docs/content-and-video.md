# Content and video

Organic short videos are the cheapest way to get customers: every sale from a free post
saves the £8–£10 ad cost per order (see [business-model.md](business-model.md)).

## Where videos can come from, legally

| Source | OK to use? | How |
|---|---|---|
| **CJ supplier videos** for the products we sell | ✅ Yes. CJ supplies them to its dropshippers to market that product (check CJ's current terms) | Already on every product page (`/api/video/[id]`). Download them from the CJ product page for social posts. Trim them, add your own captions and voice-over, and crop out other shops' watermarks or logos |
| **Your own footage** | ✅ Best option | Order 1 sample of each top-10 product from CJ (costs money, so the owner decides) and film 10–20 short clips on a phone: unboxing, before/after, "3 ways to use" |
| **Creators you pay or gift** (UGC) | ✅ With a written licence | Send a free product to small creators (TikTok Creator Marketplace, Instagram, Fiverr / Billo-style UGC platforms). Agree in writing that you may use the video in ads, and for how long |
| **Spark Ads / Partnership ads** | ✅ With the creator's permission code | TikTok Spark Ads and Instagram Partnership ads run a creator's *own* post as an ad, with their authorisation |
| **Stock footage** | ✅ Within the licence | Pexels / Pixabay (free licence), or Storyblocks / Envato (paid), for lifestyle B-roll |
| **AI-generated scenes or voice-over** | ✅ If it doesn't mislead | Fine for backgrounds, text animations and voice-overs. Never fake a "real customer" or show the product doing something it can't |
| **Other people's TikTok, YouTube or Instagram videos** of the same product | ❌ **No** | Downloading and re-posting someone else's video is copyright infringement, even of the same product. It breaks the platforms' terms, and it gets ad accounts and pages banned. You may **watch** them for ideas (hooks, angles, what's trending) and **link** to or **duplicate/stitch** them using the platform's own features, but never re-upload them as your own |

> Why the store doesn't automatically "grab" videos from other websites: besides the
> copyright problem, TikTok and YouTube forbid automated downloading in their terms,
> and posting a video that's already viral elsewhere is often detected as unoriginal,
> which gets almost no reach. Original or licensed clips perform better *and* are safe.

## What to post: 4 formats that sell gadgets

1. **Problem → solution (7–15 s).** Show the annoying moment, then the product fixing it. Text hook in the first second: "Pet hair on everything?"
2. **Satisfying demo (5–10 s).** The product working in one clean shot, with a trending sound from the platform's commercial library.
3. **"3 ways to use" (15–25 s).** Quick cuts with numbered captions.
4. **Unboxing / first impressions (20–30 s).** Builds trust; good for higher-priced items.

**Every post includes:**
- a clear hook in the first second;
- the product name;
- the price in the caption or on screen;
- a call to action: "link in bio", or a shop link.

Use the platform's **commercial music library** for business accounts. Popular songs
aren't licensed for brand use.

## A simple weekly plan

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | Problem → solution: hero product 1 |
| Tue | Satisfying demo: add-on product |
| Wed | 3 ways to use: hero product 2 |
| Thu | Problem → solution: pets category |
| Fri | Unboxing: higher-priced product |
| Sat | "Our top 5 this week" carousel (photos from the shop) |
| Sun | Repost the week's best performer with a new hook |

Post the same video to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, then check which
products get clicks. Those are the ones to run small paid tests on.

## Turning a CJ video into a post (10 minutes)

1. Download the supplier video from the CJ product page. The same clips play on our product page.
2. In CapCut or a similar editor:
   - trim to 7–15 seconds;
   - crop or blur any other shop's watermark;
   - add your text hook and captions;
   - add a commercial-library sound or your own voice-over.
3. End card: product name, price, and "Brightpan: link in bio".
4. Caption: one line of benefit plus 3–5 relevant hashtags (#kitchengadgets #cleaninghacks #doglife …).

## Captions and claims: the same rules as the site

- No health, medical or safety claims, and no "eco", "non-toxic" or "kills 99% of germs" unless proven.
- Don't say "viral on TikTok" unless it actually was for *our* listing.
- No fake reviews, testimonials or invented sales numbers. These are illegal under the UK DMCC Act 2024 and the US FTC rules.
- Paid or gifted creators must label posts as ads (#ad, or the platform's paid-partnership tag).

## Ads, when you're ready (spending money: owner's decision)

- Start with the products that got the best organic engagement.
- Test 3 products × £10/day for 4–5 days each. Stop any test whose cost per purchase is above £8 (UK) or $10 (US); those are the figures built into the pricing.
- Use catalogue ads later: a product-feed export for Meta and Google Shopping is on the [automation roadmap](automation.md#roadmap-more-automation-worth-building).
